                        DESIGN RATIONALE
================================================================================

Why Microphone?
✓ Instantly recognizable as voice/speech related
✓ Professional and business-appropriate
✓ Universal symbol for recording and speaking
✓ Modern and clean design
✓ Matches the app's core functionality

Why Sound Waves?
✓ Shows audio/voice activity
✓ Adds visual interest
✓ Reinforces the speech analysis theme
✓ Creates sense of motion/activity

Why Gradient Background?
✓ Modern and eye-catching
✓ Matches the app's color scheme
✓ Creates depth and dimension
✓ Professional appearance
